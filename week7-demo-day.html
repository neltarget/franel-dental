01THE PROBLEM· 30 SECONDS

Dental clinics lose money from empty chairs

How patients actually reach out

Enquiries land on WhatsApp — after hours, on weekends — when the front desk can't be there to answer fast enough.

What happens next

An unanswered message doesn't ping back. The conversation dies — and a patient who tried once and got silence books the clinic that answered.

The cost

Every empty chair is a fee that never arrives — revenue the clinic lost before the patient ever sat down.

03HOW FRANEL HELPS DR OSEI· 2 MINUTES · LIVE

A day in the life of Dr Osei

After hours

A patient messages at any hour. Franel responds in seconds and books an available slot in Dr Osei's calendar — no double-booking, no guesswork.

Before & after the visit

A pre-visit reminder with everything the patient needs to know — and a check-in after the visit, so care continues past the chair.

The dashboard

Dr Osei tracks bookings, attendance, and no-shows. When a patient asks something Franel shouldn't guess about — insurance for implants, say — it hands the conversation to him with the full context.

Insight for planning

He can even ask Franel which services patients want the most — and use that insight to plan.
